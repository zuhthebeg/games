# Monster skills, knight upgrades and wand shield — approved design
User30636 proposed, user30644 approved parent design. No new approval needed for normal implementation/test/deploy.
## Scope
Skeleton-only books: poison field, curse, invisibility. Knight skills: sword wave, two-handed melee berserker. Human one-handed wand+shield usability using existing wand items rather than duplicate weapon family. Skeleton lineage weapon restrictions unchanged. No new skeleton mage/archer playable classes. Existing items, save IDs, recipes, gear restrictions and spells preserved.
## Skills
Poison field is finite targeted enemy-only periodic damage with mastery-grown radius and duration. Curse temporarily reduces enemy damage and armor (actual damage calculation); bounded magnitude/duration, no multiplicative infinite stacking. Invisible removes hostile auto-target selection, not invulnerability or removal from world: already-fired/area hits can hit; attack/other skill/useful damaging hit breaks, owner sees ghost silhouette and enemy/remote views must not leak silhouette/name/target marker. Buff expiration/death/leave cleanly restore states. Invisibility is no indefinite stealth, blocks neither movement nor manual untargeted area attacks. Existing autoattack must not instantly cancel stealth absent intentional attack command.
Sword wave is directional traveling piercing slash, mastery extends range/unique targets. Available to eligible sword forms (one/two handed), not bows/wands/blunt. Berserker two-handed blade-family sword/blunt only: timed outgoing power/attack speed boost with explicit increased incoming damage; no auto healing or permanent stat changes. Weapon swap cannot retain incompatible buff advantage. All five natural casts spend MP/CD and mastery once per cast and snapshot growth at cast start.
## Growth and access
Use existing canonical skillGrowth milestones1/5/10/15/20/25/30, max30 and unchanged XP thresholds. Set bounded conservative numerical defaults in shared data and document current/next UI from same data. Existing skill stats not rebalanced. Monster books visible/learnable/equippable/castable only to actual skeletal lineage; temporary human bone-set transformation does not grant permanent access. Runtime host validates allowed skills and lineage from verified profile. Knight books follow existing upper STR45 purchase pattern and family/form restrictions. Five skills accessible through ordinary shops/skill UI, not debug-only. Costs can match existing 250–360 advanced books without global economy overhaul.
## Wand+shield
Current wand entries already exist with handedness1: preserve IDs, names and saved stats; ensure focus-family is no longer blanket excluded from shield equip/hold/timing/parry graphics when actual one-handed wand. Two-handed weapons remain shield-incompatible. Do not rebalance all staffs or change existing handedness just to create cosmetic differentiation. Clearly label one-handed wand and shield availability. Equip atomicity and reload validation preserved.
